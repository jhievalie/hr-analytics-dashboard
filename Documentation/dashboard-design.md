# Dashboard Design

## Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard was designed to provide HR professionals and business leaders with a clear overview of workforce metrics, employee attrition, compensation, and demographic trends.

The dashboard follows an executive-style layout that emphasizes readability, consistency, and actionable business insights.

---

# Design Principles

The dashboard was designed using the following principles:

- Clean and modern interface
- Consistent visual hierarchy
- Business-first storytelling
- Interactive filtering
- Minimal visual clutter
- Responsive spacing and alignment

---

# Layout

The dashboard is organized into four logical sections.

## Executive Summary

Located at the top of the report.

Includes four KPI cards:

- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Age

These KPIs provide an immediate snapshot of workforce health.

---

## Employee Attrition Analysis

This section focuses on employee turnover.

Visuals include:

- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role

These visuals help identify departments and roles with the highest employee turnover.

---

## Workforce & Compensation Analysis

This section provides demographic and compensation insights.

Visuals include:

- Gender Distribution
- Average Salary by Department

These charts help HR compare workforce composition and salary distribution across departments.

---

## Workforce Insights

This section highlights factors that may influence employee retention.

Visuals include:

- Overtime Impact on Attrition
- Attrition Rate by Age Group

These insights help decision-makers identify patterns associated with employee turnover.

---

# Color Palette

A consistent color palette was used to improve readability and reinforce meaning.

| Purpose | Color |
|---------|--------|
| Background | #F5F7FA |
| KPI Cards | #FFFFFF |
| Primary Text | #1F2937 |
| Secondary Text | #6B7280 |
| Attrition | #EF4444 |
| Workforce | #2563EB |
| Compensation | #10B981 |
| Age Analysis | #F59E0B |

The color scheme allows users to quickly distinguish between different analytical sections.

---

# Typography

Font:

- Segoe UI

Hierarchy:

- Dashboard Title
- Section Titles
- KPI Values
- Chart Labels

This hierarchy improves readability while maintaining a professional appearance.

---

# Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- Department
- Gender
- Job Role
- Business Travel
- Marital Status

All report visuals respond dynamically to slicer selections, enabling focused analysis.

---

# Business Storytelling

The dashboard follows a question-driven analytical flow:

1. How many employees are in the organization?
2. What is the overall attrition rate?
3. Which departments experience the highest turnover?
4. Which job roles are most affected?
5. How is compensation distributed?
6. Does overtime influence attrition?
7. Which age groups have the highest attrition?

This progression allows users to move from high-level KPIs to detailed workforce insights.

---

# Design Goals

The dashboard was designed to:

- Deliver key HR metrics at a glance
- Support data-driven HR decisions
- Highlight employee retention trends
- Encourage interactive exploration
- Maintain a clean executive-style presentation
